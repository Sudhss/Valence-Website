----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  BENCHMARK 7: End-to-End Keystroke Cost (The Resume Number)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Method: Simulate what happens on EVERY KEYSTROKE in the actual editor:
    1. insertChar into TextBuffer
    2. recordInsert into UndoManager
    3. rebuildCommentState (tokenize ALL lines)
    4. tokenize visible lines (~50) for rendering
  This is the REAL critical path. QPainter rendering is NOT included
  (that requires the Qt event loop and is measured separately via QElapsedTimer).

  Doc Size  Insert (ns)     Undo Rec (ns)   Rebuild (us)    Render (us)     Total (us)
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  100         56              250             42.1            22.1            64.4
  500         65              274             201.5           32.1            234.0
  1000        88              422             415.7           20.3            436.6
  5000        265             1740            2552.9          25.4            2580.3
  10000       464             2815            8660.9          40.2            8704.5

  NOTE: QPainter rendering typically adds ~3-5ms on top of these numbers.
  Total end-to-end = above + QPainter = the number you put on your resume.
